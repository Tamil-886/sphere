Safety, Supervision & Medical Care

Every child's physical safety and emotional well-being is safeguarded with rigorous medical protocols and caring mentorship.

  1. Home
  2. Safety

Ultra-Low 1:5 Ratio

We exceed industry standards with a 1:4 ratio for Mini Explorers and 1:5 for Juniors and Teens. No camper is ever unattended.

Background Screening

100% of staff undergo comprehensive FBI & state criminal background checks, sex offender registry screening, and drug testing.

24/7 Registered Nurses

Our air-conditioned on-campus Health Lodge is staffed by registered pediatric nurses for allergy management and daily medications.

PIN Secured Dismissal

Campers are only released to authorized guardians who provide a verified pickup PIN code and matching government photo ID.

Nut-Free & Allergy Safe

Our kitchen is 100% peanut and tree-nut free. We provide specialized gluten-free, dairy-free, and vegan meal prep with color-coded trays.

Red Cross Lifeguards

All aquatic activities feature Red Cross certified water safety instructors and lifeguards with buddy checks every 10 minutes.

Emergency Readiness

Prepared for Any Situation

CampSphere maintains direct dispatch coordination with the Lake Tahoe Fire Protection District and Barton Memorial Hospital (located 6 miles away).

Daily Medication Protocol

Prescription medications must be submitted in original pharmacy containers and are stored in locked medical fridges administered exclusively by RNs.

Severe Weather & Lightning Radar

Indoor gymnasium and STEM labs equipped to immediately transition outdoor programs in the event of rain or excessive summer heat index.

Emotional Care & Anti-Bullying

Zero-tolerance for exclusionary behavior or bullying. Counselors are certified in youth restorative conflict resolution and positive psychology.

Parent Inquiries

Safety & Health FAQs

Common questions about our medical staff, allergy procedures, and daily check-in protocols.

CampSphere is an entirely nut-free campus. Outside food containing nuts is prohibited. Our kitchen staff prepares customized allergy-safe meals on sanitized separate equipment, and campers with EpiPens have emergency packs carried by their dedicated counselor.

All staff are at least 19 years old (or 18 with 2 years youth leadership experience) and undergo thorough FBI fingerprint background checks, reference reviews, and 40 hours of intensive pre-season safety training including CPR, AED, and mental health first aid.

Our on-site Registered Nurse immediately evaluates the camper in our Health Lodge. Parents receive an immediate direct phone call for any fever, head injury, or significant symptom, along with a written health incident log in the parent portal.

Upon registration, parents generate a unique 4-digit dismissal PIN and can register up to 4 authorized pickup individuals with matching photo IDs. Counselors verify this code at the carline or check-out gate before releasing any camper.
