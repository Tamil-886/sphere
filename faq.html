1. Enrollment & Registration

Registration opens February 1 for returning families and February 15 for new families. Specialty tracks like STEM Robotics and High-Ropes have strict 20-camper limits and typically fill by late April.

Yes! You can transfer sessions or switch specialty tracks up to 7 days before your session begins at zero additional fee, subject to seat availability.

2. Daily Camp Life & Nutrition

Yes! Every camper receives a fresh, hot chef-prepared lunch and two organic fruit/snack breaks daily in our dining hall. We cater to nut-free, dairy-free, gluten-free, and vegan diets.

Standard camp runs 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM. Early Bird drop-off begins at 7:30 AM (+$35/wk) and Late Afternoon Club runs until 6:00 PM (+$45/wk).

3. Payments, Refunds & Discounts

We offer a 100% full money-back guarantee for cancellations made up to 14 days before your registered session starts. Medical cancellations with doctor documentation are refunded at 100% anytime.

Yes! Official receipts with our Federal Tax ID number (EIN) can be downloaded anytime directly from your Parent Dashboard for FSA/HSA and Child Care Tax Credit filing.
