Ages 4 – 6 Years

Mini Explorers: Wonder, Play & Sensory Discovery

A nurturing, gentle introduction to day camp designed specifically for preschoolers and kindergarteners.

With our lowest 1:4 staff ratio, Mini Explorers enjoy a balanced routine of sensory water play, early nature hunts, finger painting, music circles, and peaceful air-conditioned rest periods.

1:4 Counselor Ratio
Half & Full Day Options
Shaded Wading Pool Play
Quiet Nap / Story Nooks

Ages 7 – 10 Years

Junior Adventurers: Skills, Teamwork & STEM

The prime age for building self-confidence, trying new sports, and building working robots!

Junior Adventurers rotate between hands-on LEGO robotics, swimming lessons in our Olympic pool, soccer tournaments, clay pottery sculpting, and exciting nature trail hikes.

1:5 Counselor Ratio

Ages 11 – 15 Years

Teen Champions: High Adventure, Python & Leadership

Thrill-seeking outdoor challenges, real programming, and meaningful friendships that last a lifetime.

Middle and early high school campers tackle our 40-foot high-ropes course, learn Python game development, compete in athletic bracket championships, and experience optional Thursday campfire sleepovers.

Ages 16 – 18 Years

Leader-in-Training (LIT): Real Mentorship & Credentials

Preparing high schoolers for college applications, career leadership, and professional counselor roles.

LITs receive official American Red Cross CPR/First Aid certification, shadow experienced senior counselors, coordinate camp rallies, and earn up to 40 community service hours per week.
