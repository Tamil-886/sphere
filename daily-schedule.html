Frequently asked questions about single-day passes, daily drop-off, and gear.

Standard drop-off runs from 8:30 AM to 9:00 AM at the Main Gate Carline. Pick-up is from 3:30 PM to 4:00 PM. Optional Early Bird (7:30 AM) and After-Camp Club (until 6:00 PM) can be selected during checkout.
