About CampSphere

  1. Home

Founded in 2011

Empowering Kids to Explore, Create & Grow with Confidence

CampSphere was founded with a single belief: every child deserves a screen-free summer filled with boundless curiosity, supportive mentorship, and exhilarating challenges.

Across our 50-acre pine woodland campus in Lake Tahoe, we combine the joy of traditional campfires and nature hikes with cutting-edge STEM robotics labs, certified Olympic swim instruction, and expressive fine arts studios.

Our Guiding Pillars

The CampSphere Core Values

Every program, counselor training session, and daily game is rooted in four essential character principles.

Safety & Inclusion

An emotionally welcoming and physically secure sanctuary where every child feels seen, celebrated, and supported.

Curiosity & Growth

Encouraging calculated risk-taking, hands-on experimentation, and resilience through constructive trial and error.

Teamwork & Empathy

Building cooperative friendships, active listening skills, and kindness that extends far beyond camp season.

Environmental Care

Instilling deep respect for nature, wildlife stewardship, and sustainable leave-no-trace outdoor ethics.

State-Of-The-Art Campus

Our 50-Acre Outdoor & Lab Facilities

Purpose-built facilities engineered for safe sports, immersive STEM discovery, and creative arts.

Sports Complex & Fields

Natural turf soccer pitches, covered basketball pavilion, archery ranges, and track & field arenas.

STEM & Robotics Innovation Hub

High-speed workstations, 3D printers, LEGO Spike kits, Arduino sensors, and drone flight cages.

Wilderness Trails & Amphitheater

Over 6 miles of scenic forested trails, rock climbing towers, and our 300-seat evening campfire bowl.
